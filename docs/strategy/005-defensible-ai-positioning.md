# 005 — "Defensible AI": the external frame for the Record

**Date:** 2026-09-16 · **Status:** Positioning note, adopted from the Gemini memo's one good marketing idea (strategy 003 §5.1) · **Applies to:** pitch, OM §II/§III, website copy, insurer and bar conversations. **Does not apply to the UI**, which stays discreet per the 09-10 aesthetics rule ("the matter's Record", never "audit log"/"compliance").

## The sentence

> When your malpractice carrier asks how you used AI on a matter, or a court or the bar asks what you verified, Contextspaces hands you the record: which model read what, under what retention terms, which citations a named attorney verified and when, and what left the matter, to whom.

## Why it sells (and is true)

- **The insurer is a buyer.** The "Silent AI" gap is real: sanctions are excluded from professional liability policies, and carriers are adding AI endorsements and asking about AI use on renewal. A record that distinguishes ordinary negligence (covered) from unverified abdication (excluded) is worth money to the insured. This is W1 (ledger) + W5 (the Record) + W6 (citation verification).
- **The bar is a buyer.** New Jersey's mandatory technology CLE from 2027 and California SB 574 (if signed) turn "reasonable steps to verify" and "personally verified" citations from advice into duties. A platform that produces the verification trail answers the question before it is asked.
- **The client is a buyer.** Outside-counsel security questionnaires ask about hosting, subprocessors and retention. The Record's per-session retention terms answer them in one attachment.

## What we can say, and what we cannot

Say: no third party outside our (or the firm's) accounts received sealed content; every model that processed it ran under a zero-retention mode verified from the provider's catalog; here is who verified each citation and when; here is what left the matter.

Do not say: that the platform "preserves" or "guarantees" privilege (privilege attaches through counsel direction and confidentiality, not software); that any statute is "complied with" by using the product; that SB 574 is law (it awaits the Governor as of 09-16). Per the drafting rule: nothing Eden would not stand behind if the sentence were forwarded.

## Order of the pitch (unchanged from 08-25, sharpened)

1. Egress enforcement at the data (the seal). 2. Runaway-agent containment (server-side refusal). 3. **The Record** (this note). 4. Enforced discipline over remembered discipline. Model neutrality runs underneath all four: the answer to model risk is neutrality plus a verifiable corpus plus a human reviewer, not excluding the most capable models from the room (OM verification thesis; strategy 003 §6).
